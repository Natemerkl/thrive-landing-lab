## Troubleshooting

### My dashboard won't load

Try these steps:
1. Clear your browser cache and cookies
2. Try a different browser
3. Check your internet connection
4. Disable browser extensions temporarily
5. Contact support if the issue persists

### I can't upload my payment receipt

Common solutions:
1. Ensure file size is under 10MB
2. Use supported formats (JPG, PNG, PDF)
3. Try a different browser
4. Check your internet connection
5. Contact support for alternative upload methods

### I'm not receiving email notifications

Check these items:
1. Verify your email address in account settings
2. Check your spam/junk folder
3. Add our email to your contacts
4. Ensure your email provider isn't blocking our emails
5. Update your email preferences in settings

### The website looks different on my phone

This might be normal responsive behavior. However, if something seems wrong:
1. Clear your mobile browser cache
2. Try viewing in different mobile browsers
3. Check if it's a temporary loading issue
4. Contact us with screenshots if the issue persists
